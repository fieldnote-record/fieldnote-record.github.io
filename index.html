Vinyl to WAV

Plug in the turntable. Press record.

Run a cable from your record player's headphone or line-out jack into your computer, pick that input below, and record a side. When you press End, the whole side is raised to one even level and saved as a lossless WAV in this browser.

Most computer headphone jacks are output only. If yours won't hear the turntable, use a USB audio adapter with a mic or line-in jack, or the turntable's own USB port.

Record a side

Drop the needle, then press record

Choose the input your turntable is plugged into and drop the needle. Check that the meters move, then press Record just before the first track and End after the run-out groove.

Set the turntable's volume so the loudest parts peak below 0 dB. The meter turns terracotta when it clips, and leveling can't repair clipped audio.

Input volume

Looking for the Mac app

Install Fieldnote Companion to adjust your Mac's input volume from this page. Paste this into Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://fieldnote-record.github.io/companion/install.sh | bash

A record icon appears in your menu bar. Click it to see a pairing code. Works in Chrome and Edge; if Chrome asks to let this site connect to devices on your network, allow it.

Saved in this browser

Your recordings

Recordings stay in this browser on this computer until you delete them. Download a WAV to keep a copy anywhere else.

Fieldnote Turntable Recorder records uncompressed audio from your chosen input, applies a single gain to the finished side, and saves 16-bit WAV files in this browser's storage. No audio leaves your computer.
