Vinyl to WAV

Record your turntable straight into this browser.

Your Bluetooth record player streams to an ESP32. The ESP32 passes the audio down its USB cable to this page, which saves each side as a lossless WAV file in your browser.

Step 1 · Once per board

Flash the ESP32

Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a data USB cable, then flash it. This takes about 30 seconds and only needs doing once.

Use an original ESP32 board (ESP32-WROOM-32 or ESP32-DevKitC). The ESP32-C3, S2 and S3 only have Bluetooth Low Energy and can't receive turntable audio.

Fieldnote Recorder firmware 1.0.0 

Waiting to start. Chrome will ask which serial port to use; pick the one named CP210x, CH340, CH9102 or "USB Serial".

If flashing stalls at "Connecting", hold the board's BOOT button while you click Flash, and let go once the progress bar moves.

Step 2 · Once per turntable

Pair your turntable

After flashing, the ESP32 acts like a Bluetooth speaker. It reconnects to the same turntable automatically each time it powers up.

  1. Keep the ESP32 plugged into this computer by USB. It's powered from the cable.
  2. Put the turntable into Bluetooth pairing mode. Most have a Bluetooth button you hold for three seconds.
  3. The turntable should find Fieldnote Recorder and connect. If yours pairs to the nearest speaker on its own, turn off your other Bluetooth speakers first.
  4. Connect below. The signal chain at the top turns green when the turntable is connected.

Step 3 · Every side

Record a side

Connect, drop the needle and watch the meter. Click record a moment before the first track, and stop after the run-out groove.

Keep the turntable's own volume at maximum; the ESP32 records the stream before any volume change, so peaks stay clean either way.
